ABOUT US

The People Behind Your Next Hire

SVME Enterprises is a Tirupati-based business development and HR consultancy built around one idea: hiring shouldn't be complicated. We help growing businesses find people who genuinely fit — and stay involved long after the offer letter is signed.

We work as an extension of your team, not just a vendor. From understanding what a role actually needs to sourcing, screening, and onboarding the right person, we stay close to the process so nothing falls through the cracks.
